## 01. Summary & Attention Tunnel Geometry

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **TETRIS COGNITIVE GEOMETRY TRILOGY · PART 2 OF 3**  
> This specification documents the **granular 13-topic taxonomy** and the **interactive 10×10 Spoon Energy Canvas**. It builds directly on the macro diurnal mode containers in **[Part 1: The Six Diurnal Mode Blocks](/six-diurnal-mode-blocks/)**, and provides the empirical basis for the frontier reasoning analyses synthesized in **[Part 3: Fable's Cognitive Geometric Rationales](/topic-tetris-shape-geometry/)**.

For an AuDHD monotropic operator, human activities are not fungible chronological units. They are **geometric cognitive shapes**. 

Standard productivity frameworks (time-blocking, Pomodoro, calendar slicing) fail catastrophically for neurodivergent minds because they presume zero friction between adjacent schedule intervals. In clock-time models, an hour of deep curriculum architecture is treated as mathematically equivalent and mechanically interchangeable with an hour of partner email outreach or a group video conference.

Under **Monotropic Attention Tunnel Geometry**, attention is a high-inertia directional beam with substantial entry velocity and high state-preservation overhead. Different activities demand fundamentally incompatible cognitive shapes:
1. **Linear Monotropic Tunnels (I-Pieces)**: Rigid, deep, narrow channels that require total immersion. Once entered, the cost of interruption is catastrophic state evaporation.
2. **Branching Stubs (T-Pieces)**: Anchored command trunks with lateral exploratory nodes.
3. **Omni-Directional Social Demands (Cross-Pieces)**: High multi-channel perceptual monitoring requiring continuous masking and sensory defense.
4. **Grounding Slabs & Boxes (O-Pieces)**: Self-contained, closed-loop somatic containers that ground the operator and reset cognitive arousal.

When an operator attempts to drop an I-piece directly onto a Cross-piece, the shapes physically cannot mate. The resulting misalignment produces **structural air gaps**—unaccounted cognitive voids where working memory crashes, executive paralysis sets in, and recovery latency consumes 30 to 60 minutes.

This specification documents the **13 Canonical Topic Blocks** synthesized from over 100 raw activity log entries from founder Bryce Hepburn, defines the mathematical rules of seam friction and air gap physics, and establishes the operating manual for the interactive simulation engine.

## 03. Seam Incompatibility & Air Gap Physics

The core principle of Topic-Based Tetris is that **some shapes cannot touch without creating an air gap**. When an air gap forms on the schedule matrix, cognitive state leaks into the void.

```
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| BLOCK A (SOURCE)  | BLOCK B (TARGET)  | SEAM VERDICT      |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Course Authoring  | Social / Meetings | FATAL 3-CELL VOID |
| (I-Piece)         | (Cross-Piece)     | (State Wipeout)   |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Gym Conditioning  | Course Authoring  | CLEAN DOCK        |
| (2x2 Square)      | (I-Piece)         | (Zero Friction)   |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Domestic Base     | Any Work Block    | UNIVERSAL BASE    |
| (L-Piece)         |                   | (Stable Anchor)   |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Vibe Coding       | Course Authoring  | HOOK TRAP         |
| (J-Piece)         | (I-Piece)         | (Double Debt)     |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
| Admin / Banking   | Inbox Triage      | FLUSH SLAB        |
| (1x4 Slab)        | (1x2 Notch)       | (Zero Debt)       |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+
```

### Seam Physics Rules:

1. **The I-Piece / Cross-Piece Destruction Rule**:
   Stacking an `I-Piece` directly onto a `Cross-Piece` leaves an irregular multi-cell void. In human terms: exiting a 4-hour instructional design sprint and opening a 10-person video conference immediately triggers severe brain freeze, verbal masking stammer, and working-memory collapse. 
   - **Remedy**: A minimum 15-minute `Somatic Notch (+)` buffer or a quiet outdoor walk is mathematically mandatory between these blocks.

2. **The 2x2 Clean Ground Rule**:
   The `O-Square` (Gym) has no irregular edges. Stacking an `I-Piece` (Course Authoring) onto an `O-Square` creates a clean, flush horizontal seam. Physical exertion empties mental ram, enabling rapid monotropic ignition.

3. **The J-Hook Entrapment Rule**:
   The `J-Block` (Vibe Coding) hooks around corners. Entering is effortless; exiting is resisted. If a scheduled `I-Piece` follows a `J-Piece`, the operator will blow through the boundary, compounding cognitive fatigue into complete exhaustion.
   - **Remedy**: Always place a hard terminal anchor (e.g. dinner, dog walk, or Z-inverter) after a J-block.

4. **The L-Foot Stabilization Law**:
   The `L-Piece` (Domestic Routine) provides the structural floor of the entire day. A stable 04:00 rising protocol with coffee and dog walking establishes a level base across the matrix, preventing asymmetric collapses later in the afternoon.
