## 01. Mathematical Genesis & The 24-Hour Calculation

Standard productivity systems treat 24 hours as 1,440 interchangeable minutes mapped across a single chronological line. For an AuDHD monotropic operator, this linear assumption causes chronic burnout, revenge bedtime delays, and catastrophic friction transitions.

In this model, **time is strictly prohibited as an axis**. Instead, the 24-hour cycle is modeled as an allocatable 2D capacity envelope where tasks are geometric polyominos placed according to attentional breadth and regulatory depth.

### The 70-Cell Derivation ($7 \times 10$ Grid)
- **Attentional Breadth ($X_2 = 7$ Channels)**: Seven represents the maximum sustainable simultaneous lateral threads before attentional fragmentation occurs.
- **Regulatory Depth ($Y_2 = 10$ Strata)**: Ten vertical strata representing the descent from surface renewable spoons (Rows 1–4) to deep biological reserves (Rows 7–10).
- **Total Capacity**: $7 \times 10 = 70\text{ attention cells}$.

## 03. The Three Operating Canvas Modes

### Mode A: Attentional Bandwidth $\times$ Reserve Stratum (The Density Sink)
- **$X_2$ Axis**: Left = Embodied/Substrate routines $\longleftrightarrow$ Right = Effortful/Novel demands.
- **$Y_2$ Axis**: Top = Renewable surface spoons (Rows 1–4) $\longleftrightarrow$ Bottom = Deep biological reserves (Rows 7–10).
- **Cost Gravity Mechanics**: Blocks sink according to executive cost. High-autonomy special interests float; coercive external demands sink into reserves.

### Mode B: Autonomy $\times$ Somatic Arousal (The Regulatory Field)
- **$X_2$ Axis**: Left = Self-Authored Flow $\longleftrightarrow$ Right = External Demand / Coercion.
- **$Y_2$ Axis**: Bottom = Parasympathetic Grounding $\longleftrightarrow$ Top = Noradrenergic Peak / Sensory Overload.
- **Quadrant Mapping**: Maps whether daily activities concentrate in restorative green sectors or pile up in high-arousal threat zones.

### Mode C: Channel Bandwidth $\times$ Spoon Ceiling (The Scalable Matrix)
- **$X_2$ Axis**: Simultaneous channel allocation limit (7 streams).
- **$Y_2$ Axis**: Total daily spoon capacity (10 strata).
- **Free Packing**: Fully order-independent packing to test whether a planned workload fits without spilling over into executive shutdown.

## 04. Air Gap Telemetry & Cognitive Bleed

When polyominos with incompatible geometries are packed without interstitial mortar, **trapped air gaps** emerge:
- **Mechanical Definition**: An empty grid cell enclosed on $\ge 3$ sides (or $\ge 2$ sides against the canvas boundary).
- **Cognitive Tax**: Each air gap represents **45 minutes of lost cognitive latency** (executive initiation friction, context-switching confusion, and task paralysis).
- **Target Threshold**: A sustainable 24-hour day maintains $\le 3$ air gaps. $\ge 6$ trapped gaps triggers an automatic **Spoon Bleed Alert**.
