## 01. Frontier Reasoning Genesis & The Isomorphism Principle

For an AuDHD monotropic operator, activities are not abstract durations that can be scheduled arbitrarily on a calendar. **Activities are cognitive polyominos with immutable physical geometries.**

On 2026-09-03, a dedicated battery of **13 targeted frontier reasoning queries** was dispatched to `claude-fable-5` on Replicate (~$0.08/call, $1.06 total investment). Each query subjected one of the 13 canonical operational domains to rigorous attentional analysis against founder Bryce Hepburn's longitudinal telemetry (Vyvanse 50mg pharmacodynamics, MAIA-2 interoceptive floor, EDA-QA demand avoidance, and LevelDB energy stores).

### The 4 Pillars of Geometric Isomorphism
1. **Attention Tunnel Depth (Z-Axis)**: The vertical extent of continuous working memory. Monotropic tunnels (e.g. Course Authoring `I-Block`) require holding an entire schema in suspension; partial completion yields zero shippable value.
2. **Directional Inertia (Momentum)**: The directional velocity of cognition. Linear momentum cascades straight down; creative momentum drifts sinuously (`S-Block`); branching momentum fans outward laterally (`T-Block`).
3. **Rotation Resistance**: The cognitive torque required to pivot an activity mid-flight. Activities with high rotation resistance (e.g. `J-Block` vibe coding, `I-Block` curriculum builds) suffer catastrophic wall-kick failures when forced to pivot.
4. **Seam Physics & Contact Surfaces**: How adjacent blocks interact. Compatible boundaries dock flush; incompatible boundaries generate **structural air gaps** where working memory crashes, executive paralysis sets in, and recovery latency consumes 30 to 90 minutes.
